SafeSeat Step 5.9.8.1 Arduino String overload hotfix
===================================================

Fixes ESP32 Arduino core 3.3.10 compile error:
  call of overloaded 'String(float&, uint8_t&)' is ambiguous

Change in SafeSeatApi.cpp:
  String(value, decimals)
becomes:
  String(value, static_cast<unsigned int>(decimals))

No Fusion, ESP-NOW, telemetry schema, sensor model, or camera logic was changed.
